# Earnings Play Strategy

## Overview
Position around quarterly earnings announcements using pre-earnings sentiment, historical reaction patterns, and options-implied expected moves.

## Pre-Earnings Setup (Swing into Earnings)
### Entry Criteria
- Strong fundamental trend (3+ quarters of beats)
- Analyst estimates trending higher (estimate revisions positive)
- Insider buying in the 30 days before earnings
- Technical setup: price in an uptrend, near support
- Enter 3-5 days before earnings date

### Risk Management
- Maximum position size: 3% of portfolio (reduced from standard 5%)
- Stop-loss: Below pre-earnings support level
- Accept that earnings are binary events — size accordingly

## Post-Earnings Reaction (Gap and Go)
### Entry Criteria
- Earnings beat on both revenue and EPS
- Guidance raised or maintained
- Gap up on high volume (>= 2x average)
- Price holds above VWAP after first 30 minutes
- Enter on first pullback to VWAP or intraday support

### Exit Criteria
- **Target:** Measured move equal to the gap size
- **Stop-loss:** Below the gap fill level (for gap-and-go)
- **Time stop:** If momentum fades within 2 days, close position

## Avoid
- Trading earnings on stocks with no clear fundamental trend
- Holding through earnings with oversized positions
- Trading the first 5 minutes of post-earnings reaction (too volatile)
- Stocks with very high implied volatility (IV crush risk)

## Timeframe
- Pre-earnings: 3-5 day hold into announcement
- Post-earnings: 1-5 day swing
